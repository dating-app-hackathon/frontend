## 💡 Inspiration

Dating apps often encourage us to judge people in seconds based on a photo. **SyncItUp explores a different approach:** what if we discovered someone's personality, values, interests, and vibe before seeing what they look like?

Built around the idea that meaningful compatibility goes deeper than first impressions, SyncItUp transforms dating into a journey of self-discovery and genuine connection.

## ✨ What It Does

- **Personalised Onboarding** — Share your identity, preferences, values, virtues, and story.
- **360° Body Scan** — Explore a real-time pose-detection experience.
- **AI Vibe Check** — An interactive voice interview paired with a multimodal dashboard.
- **Vibe Archetypes** — Discover a personality-inspired representation of your vibe.
- **Meaningful Syncs** — Find potential connections through shared values and compatibility signals, rather than likes and swipes.
- **Sync Map** — Explore a visual map of potential connections.
- **Interactive Experiences** — Get to know yourself and others through AI interviews, written questions, *Sync by Reel*, *Moral Mirror*, and *Memory Auction*.

## 🧠 What Makes It Different?

SyncItUp experiments with a more expressive approach to compatibility by bringing together personality, values, voice, movement, and interactive self-reflection.

The goal isn't to reduce people to a score. It's to create a more engaging starting point for conversations that matter.

> **Prototype note:** Some features, including EEG and heart-rate data, use simulated signals. The Sync Map uses seeded visualisation data rather than scientifically validated compatibility measurements. These experiences are exploratory prototypes, not validated psychological or medical assessments.

## 🚀 What's Next?

- Integrate a locally running speech-to-speech AI model.
- Replace seeded visualisations with real, thoughtfully designed compatibility embeddings.
- Explore optional wearable integrations.
- Introduce **Twin Date** and **Prediction Check** experiences.
- Help compatible people move from digital discovery to real-world group activities.
- Strengthen privacy-by-design controls and transparent consent around sensitive data.
